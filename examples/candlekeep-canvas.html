1Business problem

What problem costs money/time today?

20,000 messages/month sorted and answered manually by 10 FTEs: ~$978K/yr, first response ~4 h. Constraint: assist-only first.

fact

2Business metrics

Which business outcomes improve? When do we stop?

Primary: FRT ≤30 min. Secondary: escalation rate ≤ baseline. Timeframe: 12 weeks. Stop: if cost per request > $4.10, pause and rescope.

8Solution metrics

Quality and performance thresholds

Quality: misroute ≤10%, draft acceptance ≥60%. Performance: routing fast enough to improve FRT.

fact

3Value for customers

For [who] with [problem], we provide [value]

Customers: a fast personalized reply instead of a 4 h wait. Specialists: less manual sorting, more time for complex cases.

5Go-to-market

2–3 experienced specialists → whole support team → customer-visible scope. Gates: ≥70% of pilot use suggestions, quality thresholds hold.

9Validation strategy

Offline: golden set of 100 + human review of edge cases. Live: shadow → assist. Expand by category. Roll back if below the bar.

4Cost structure
