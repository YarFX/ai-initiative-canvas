Call recaps — open models on Token Factory?

AI Initiative Canvas · 2026-10-19 · unattended run, assumptions labelled

REFRAME → GO on a 1–2-week bake-off

As a cost move the LLM switch saves ~$60/month (~3% of MRR); transcription costs ~3–9× more and stays with AssemblyAI. Test non-inferiority on a 60-call golden set; migrate only if an open model matches GPT-4o on action items; park fine-tuning.

  • revenue ~$1,800/mo ≈ $0.90 per call
  • GPT-4o ≈ $65/mo → gpt-oss-120b ≈ $4/mo
  • transcription ≈ $225–555/mo [assumption]
  • risk: invented / misattributed action items
Pattern: Content generation with structured (JSON) action items: not RAG, not agentic

1Business problem

What problem costs money/time today?

~1,500 audio h/month (~2,000 calls) from ~120 teams at a flat $15. COGS ~$2.4–5.2 per subscriber, mostly transcription. Lever: cost reduction (weak: ~$60/mo on the LLM step).

assumption

2Business metrics

Which business outcomes improve? When do we stop?

Primary: AI COGS ≤ $3.75 per subscriber; churn ≤ baseline. Secondary: summary tickets ≤ baseline. Stop: no candidate passes H1–H2 by week 2 → stay on GPT-4o.

8Solution metrics

Quality and performance thresholds

Action-item recall ≥90%, precision ≥85%; summary ≥ GPT-4o in ≥80% of blind pairs. p95 ≤60 s per 1 h call; valid JSON ≥99%.

3Value for customers

For [who] with [problem], we provide [value]

For small-team members leaving a call with scattered commitments: a trusted recap and action list with owner and date, better than manual notes or re-listening.

assumption

5Go-to-market

Who gets it first, first value, expansion gates

Own team → 5–10 opt-in beta users → 20% canary → all. Gates: thumbs-up and edit rate within 5 pp of GPT-4o; no rise in summary tickets.

assumption

9Validation strategy

Offline → Shadow → Assist → Auto, rollback

Offline: 60-call golden set. Live: shadow on real calls → canary with GPT-4o one flag away. Roll back if edit or thumbs-down rate is +5 pp for a week.

assumption

4Cost structure

Costs, unit economics, breakpoint, ROI range

One-time 3–6 dev-days; transcription $225–555/mo + LLM $65/mo. Unit: COGS per subscriber. Breakpoint: users >18–39 h/mo. ROI: saves ~$600–720/yr, payback ~1.7–3.7 yrs.

6Product shape

Where it lives in the workflow and what it outputs

The existing product, unchanged: after each recorded call the team gets a summary and action items (owner, due date). Only the engine behind it changes.

7AI engine
